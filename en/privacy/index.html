DATA TRANSPARENCY & SECURITY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

HermesSub is a real-time game subtitle translation and multi-voice dubbing application for Windows, macOS, iPhone/iPad, and Smart TVs. No account registration is required. The app contains no advertisements, uses no third-party ad or analytics trackers, and never sells or shares your data with advertisers.

  • No account required
  • No ads or ad tracking
  • On-device OCR & API keys
  • No IP addresses stored

1. Data Processed and Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Game Video & Screen OCR: Game frames captured from your PC/Mac display or streamed over your home Wi-Fi/LAN from your PlayStation 5 via Remote Play are processed (OCR) entirely on your device. Screenshots and video frames are never uploaded to HermesSub servers.
  • Settings, Glossaries & History: App preferences, downloaded game dictionaries, character pronoun rules, subtitle history, story notes, as well as any AI API keys and PS5 pairing keys you enter are stored strictly on your device and protected using OS-native encrypted storage (Windows DPAPI on Windows, Apple Keychain on macOS and iOS).
  • Offline Processing: Pre-translated dialogue packs and downloaded offline AI voices (such as Piper or system/Apple voices) run completely offline on your device.
  • Local Network (Wi-Fi / LAN): Discovery and communication between your PC/Mac/iPhone, PlayStation 5, Smart TV (Android TV, LG webOS, Samsung Tizen), and local browser subtitle overlays remain strictly inside your home network.

2. Data Sent Externally Only When You Use Specific Features

  • AI Translation & Online Text-to-Speech (Optional): When you trigger live AI translation or online voice synthesis, only the relevant subtitle text (along with optional game/character glossary context you configured) is sent directly from your device to the provider you choose (such as Google Gemini, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Groq, Google Translate, or Microsoft Edge TTS), using your own API key where applicable. HermesSub does not proxy, inspect, or store your API keys or private AI-translated lines. Such requests are governed by the respective provider's privacy policy.
  • Community Game Dictionary Contributions (Controllable in Settings): When the anonymous source-line sharing option is enabled (on by default and toggleable anytime in Settings on Windows, macOS, and iOS) and an active game title is identified, HermesSub uploads compressed batches of newly encountered English source dialogue lines (filtered to strip HUD artifacts and obvious personal info such as emails or phone numbers) along with optional speaker labels, game identifier, and a one-way SHA-256 device hash (hwid_hash). Screenshots, AI API keys, your story notes, private translations, and PS5 pairing keys are never sent. A nightly curation job pulls these batches to deduplicate and translate them into shared per-game Community Delta Packs, and clears processed batches from the online queue immediately afterward.
  • PSN Account ID / PS5 Pairing (Optional): When fetching your PlayStation Network Account ID, you sign in directly on Sony's official authentication page (auth.api.sonyentertainmentnetwork.com). Your PSN password, OAuth tokens, and PS5 pairing keys are stored locally on your device and are never sent to or logged by HermesSub servers.
  • Smart TV Companion Installation (Optional): When installing the TV companion on your home network, the app communicates directly with official TV vendor services if required (LG Developer developer.lge.com to extend webOS Developer Mode; Samsung Tizen account/certificate endpoints for Tizen package signing).
  • Updates, Community Dictionary Packs & Voice Model Downloads (Optional): Checking for updates or downloading game packs, daily Community Delta Packs, and offline voice models connects to GitHub (github.com / raw.githubusercontent.com) and Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Like any standard HTTPS download, those third-party hosts receive your IP address; HermesSub sends no additional tracking metadata.

3. Activation & Minimal Anonymous Usage Telemetry

  • Startup Verification & Licensing: On launch and during active runtime sessions, HermesSub sends minimal technical telemetry to our edge verification service: an anonymous one-way SHA-256 device hash (hwid_hash, not a hardware serial number, name, or email), OS platform (win-x64, osx-arm64, ios-arm64), app version, random session ID, session uptime, selected dictionary/game ID (if any), license tier (free or paid plan), and an optional license key (only if entered by you).
  • Abuse Prevention & No Stored IP Addresses: Our edge infrastructure (Cloudflare) uses your IP address transiently in memory solely for rate-limiting and derives a 2-letter country code for aggregate statistics. HermesSub does not store your IP address, name, email, or personal identity in its database.

4. Website (hermessub.com)

  • No Tracking Cookies: Our website https://hermessub.com sets no advertising or tracking cookies. It only uses browser localStorage to remember your preferred display language (VI or EN).

5. Children's Privacy

  • Age Policy: HermesSub does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
