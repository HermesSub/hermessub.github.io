Everything to know before you start

Does HermesSub require a jailbroken PS5, or can it risk a PSN account ban?

No jailbreak or console modification is ever required. HermesSub never alters game files, firmware, or console memory. It reads on-screen dialogue through official Remote Play over your home network or a desktop video window, keeping your stock PS5 and PlayStation Network account completely safe.

How do live subtitles appear on a living-room Smart TV without adding controller lag?

Your DualSense controller stays paired directly to your PS5 on your primary PSN account over native HDMI with 0ms input latency. HermesSub quietly reads dialogue in the background via a secondary PSN session and renders crisp cinema subtitles natively on your LG webOS, Samsung Tizen, or Android TV.

Which platforms, consoles, and game genres does HermesSub support?

HermesSub runs on Windows 10/11 PCs, Apple Silicon Macs, Windows gaming handhelds, and PlayStation 5 (via Remote Play, chiaki-ng, or HDMI capture cards), outputting overlays on desktop monitors or Smart TVs. It works with any story-driven RPG, action-adventure, visual novel, or indie title.

Is HermesSub free to download, and how do AI translation costs work?

HermesSub is free to download and use. Pre-built community game dictionaries and offline voice models run locally at zero cost. For live AI translation of brand-new lines, you can use free translation engines or plug in your own Gemini, DeepSeek, Groq, or OpenAI API key with full control over usage.

How is HermesSub different from generic screen OCR translators?

Generic screen translators translate isolated lines out of context, breaking character tone, relationships, and lore terminology. HermesSub tracks ongoing story context, locks per-character relationship tone and glossaries, and assigns distinct AI voices to individual speakers.

Will HermesSub slow down my PC or require high internet bandwidth?

No. HermesSub is natively engineered to run alongside demanding games with minimal CPU and GPU overhead. Local PS5 video reading and Smart TV subtitle delivery stay inside your home Wi-Fi or LAN without routing video frames through external servers.
