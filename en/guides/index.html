First-time PS5 Remote Play Connection & Pairing

One-time setup only

Follow these 5 numbered steps to pair your PlayStation 5 over your home Wi-Fi or LAN.

  1. 1

    Select your PS5 console

    Open the Discovered PS5 Consoles dropdown on your network and pick your PS5.

  2. 2

    Click Sign in to Sony

    Click 1. Sign in to Sony to open your browser for PSN login (a secondary PSN account is recommended).

  3. 3

    Copy & paste the redirect URL

    Once the browser stops on the page showing 'redirect', copy the entire address bar URL and paste it into the field.

  4. 4

    Click Get ID

    Click Get ID so HermesSub automatically fills your PSN Account ID below.

  5. 5

    Enter the 8-digit pairing code & click Pair PS5

    Open the Link Device screen on your PS5 for the 8-digit code, type all 8 digits into Step B, and click Pair PS5.

Where to find the 8-digit code on PS5: Settings -> System -> Remote Play -> Link Device

How to verify pairing succeeded

Right next to your PS5 name under Discovered PS5 Consoles, the status changes to Paired.

Note: Keep the Link Device screen open on your TV until Pair PS5 completes, as each 8-digit code expires after 300 seconds.

Connect PS5 with chiaki-ng

First-time · ~2 min

Set up PS5 Remote Play through chiaki-ng and route gameplay video into HermesSub for crisp real-time subtitles.

  1. 1

    Enable Remote Play & get the 8-digit PIN on PS5

    • Enable Remote Play: From PS5 home screen, open Settings (gear icon) -> System -> Remote Play -> toggle on Enable Remote Play.
    • Get 8-digit PIN: Right below, select Link Device. TV displays an 8-digit PIN (~5 min countdown) -> Keep this screen open on TV until Step 2 finishes.
    • Wake PS5 from PC (recommended): Open Settings -> System -> Power Saving -> Features Available in Rest Mode -> enable Stay Connected to the Internet and Enable Turning On PS5 from Network.
  2. 2

    Register your PS5 in chiaki-ng

    • Same network: PC and PS5 must share one Wi-Fi or LAN router (turn off VPN, Cloudflare WARP, or 1.1.1.1 on PC first).
    • Open chiaki-ng: In HermesSub -> Play Mode & Connection -> click Open chiaki-ng (or click Releases Page beside it to grab the Portable zip, extract, and run chiaki-ng.exe).
    • Find PS5 card: Wait a few seconds for auto-discovery. If missing, check PS5 Settings -> Network -> Connection Status -> View Connection Status for IP Address (e.g. 192.168.1.73), then click + in chiaki-ng and enter it.
    • Register: Double-click (or right-click -> Register) the PS5 card:
    • PSN Account ID: Do NOT type your display nickname! Click Login with PSN to sign into your active PS5 PlayStation Network account so chiaki-ng auto-fills your Account ID.
    • PIN: Type the 8-digit code shown on your TV from Step 1 and click Register (next time, just double-click the PS5 card to stream).
  3. 3

    Tune chiaki-ng for sharp text & smooth FPS

    Click the Settings (gear) icon in top-right of chiaki-ng -> Video tab and set:

    • Resolution & FPS: Set Resolution: 1080p and FPS: 60 for the crispest dialogue edges for HermesSub.
    • Bitrate (Recommended): Set 30000 kbps when using wired LAN or strong 5GHz Wi-Fi so dialogue text stays razor-sharp during fast motion (lower to 15000-20000 kbps if streaming over weaker Wi-Fi).
    • Video Codec: Choose H.265 (HEVC) for sharpest text in motion (if an older PC stutters, switch to H.264).
  4. 4

    Route gameplay into HermesSub for live subtitles

    • Open stream: Double-click your PS5 card in chiaki-ng to open the live stream window.
    • HermesSub setup: Open Play Mode & Connection -> choose Play on PC Screen -> under Capture Method, pick PC Game / chiaki-ng Window.
    • Pick chiaki-ng window: Under Open Game Window select chiaki-ng and keep Auto-switch to borderless checked (do NOT press F11 in chiaki-ng because exclusive fullscreen hides overlay subtitles).
    • Start: Click Start Translating at the top (optionally use Subtitle Region Alt+R to box dialogue for faster, cleaner OCR).

Quick first-time troubleshooting

  • PS5 not showing in chiaki-ng: Verify PC and PS5 share one router (not Guest Wi-Fi), disable VPN / WARP / proxy, and allow chiaki-ng on Private & Public networks in Windows Security -> Firewall & network protection -> Allow an app through firewall.
  • Invalid PIN / Register failed: 8-digit PINs expire after ~5 minutes. Reopen Settings -> System -> Remote Play -> Link Device on PS5 for a fresh code and confirm Login with PSN uses the active PS5 user.
  • Subtitles don't appear over chiaki-ng: Exit exclusive fullscreen by pressing F11 again inside chiaki-ng, then click Start Translating in HermesSub so it switches to Borderless automatically.
Tip: To skip a separate chiaki-ng install completely, choose Play PS5 directly in App (1080p60 over LAN) under Capture Method for 1-step built-in pairing.

Daily PS5 Play on Smart TV (After First-Time Pairing)

Daily · 30 sec

Once secondary PSN pairing is done, switch your controller back to your primary PSN for good. Every play session takes just 4 quick steps with 0ms TV latency and 2 pitfalls to avoid.

  1. 1

    After first-time pairing: Switch back to your primary PSN

    • Right after pairing your secondary PSN in HermesSub or chiaki-ng, press the PS button on your controller -> choose Switch User -> return to your primary PSN.
    • From then on, you never need to sign into the secondary PSN on your controller again.
  2. 2

    Step 1: Turn on PS5 & open your game on primary PSN

    • Power on your PS5, sign in with your DualSense on your primary PSN, and open your game on screen.
    • Do not leave the console on the User lock screen or with display off, otherwise Remote Play will be blocked with error 0x80108b0b.
  3. 3

    Step 2: Open HermesSub on your Smart TV

    • Launch HermesSub on your Smart TV (LG webOS, Samsung Tizen, or Android TV).
    • Select the HDMI input connected to your PS5 (if needed, press number keys 1-4 on the TV remote or press Back to pick the HDMI port in the control panel).
    • Your TV now shows the direct PS5 feed (still on your primary PSN) with 0ms latency.
  4. 4

    Steps 3 & 4: Start Translating on HermesSub & play normally

    • In HermesSub (PC / Mac / iPhone), select Smart TV subtitle output mode and click Start Translating.
    • HermesSub silently uses the secondary PSN via background Remote Play to read dialogue and audio. Your TV keeps your primary PSN game screen untouched, briefly showing Connected using Remote Play in the corner as Vietnamese subtitles appear on TV.
    • Keep playing normally with your controller on your primary PSN.
  5. 5

    2 common mistakes to avoid

    • Mistake 1 (code 11): Switching your controller to the secondary PSN. The moment you press the PS button or sign your controller into the same secondary PSN used by Remote Play, PS5 kicks HermesSub off to hand control back to the pad. Always keep your controller on your primary PSN.
    • Mistake 2 (0x80108b10 / code 4): Running another Remote Play app at the same time. PS5 only allows 1 active Remote Play session at a time. Fully close chiaki-ng or the official PS Remote Play app before clicking Start Translating in HermesSub.
Quick tip: If you accidentally switched to the secondary PSN or left chiaki-ng open, simply switch your controller back to your primary PSN, close chiaki-ng, and click Start Translating again.
